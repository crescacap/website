HT

Founder & Partner

Heloise Taillet

Heloise is a Registered Representative and Partner at Cresca Capital. She has broad experience across biotech, venture capital, and M&A, working with investors, founders, and operators to structure deals, raise capital, and execute strategic transactions and operational projects. She brings a combination of deal execution experience, an active institutional and biotech investor network, and hands-on operating experience inside boutique advisory, venture capital, and family offices.

Recent engagements include leading diligence on licensing opportunities for a hub-and-spoke biotech company, managing the acquisition of a Stanford IP-backed asset for a biotech investment firm, structuring the launch of a biotech-focused hedge fund, coordinating underwriter selection for a late-stage startup preparing for IPO, and advising several biotech companies on capital markets strategy, and clinical and commercialization planning for clients spanning investment banks, hedge funds, and both clinical and pre-clinical stage companies.

Prior to these roles, Heloise led the diligence on acquisition and investment opportunities at Saltwater Capital, the family office of Ryan Graves, Uber's first CEO. Before that, she sourced and evaluated companies with over $20M in revenue as part of the M&A team at a high-growth unicorn startup with $3B in funding. Earlier in her career, she executed investments as an investor at Secocha Ventures, an early-stage venture capital firm. She started her career as a Summer Analyst at Bank of America and Deutsche Bank.

Heloise holds a Bachelor's degree in Economics-Mathematics from Columbia College, Columbia University in New York.

VM

Principal Advisor

Dr. Victoria Meissner

Dr. Meissner is a physician–scientist, biotech investment banker and experienced biotech executive with broad expertise across oncology, immunology, infectious diseases, metabolic disorders and other life sciences sectors. She brings a rare combination of deep scientific knowledge, capital markets insights, broad institutional investor network and a strong track record in corporate strategy.

Previously, Victoria served as Global Head of Investor Relations & Vice President Strategy at BioNTech (BNTX), where she led the transformation of the company's external narrative — successfully repositioning it from a COVID-19 vaccine biotech to a global oncology leader.

Prior to BioNTech, Victoria worked at Jefferies in investment banking advising biotech clients on financings (PIPE, Follow-On, Private Placement, IPO) and M&A.

Earlier, Victoria took her academic journey to Chicago after earning her M.D. from the Medical University of Vienna. Her Ph.D. research at the University of Illinois Hospital at Chicago, div. of Transplant Surgery, focused on diabetes and pancreatic islet transplantation.

Dr. Meissner's core strengths are her extensive knowledge of biotech capital markets and her ability to leverage her subject matter expertise to translate deep scientific insight and understanding of the competitive landscape into actionable corporate strategy and compelling external positioning, often driving meaningful shifts in valuation and market perception.
